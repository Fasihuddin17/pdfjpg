Why Convert PDF to JPG?

JPG images are easy to insert into slides, post on social media, attach to forms that reject PDFs, or preview in chat apps. Converting a PDF page to JPG is useful for sharing a single page, making thumbnails, or archiving a flyer as an image.

PDF to JPG vs PDF to image

"PDF to image" can mean PNG, WebP or JPG. JPG gives small files and works everywhere, which suits photos and mixed pages. PNG is lossless and better for sharp text or line art, but files are larger. Rendered pages are raster images, so text is no longer selectable.
